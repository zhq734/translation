Document Translation

Open a dedicated workspace to compare a local document with its translation and export a layout-preserving PDF.

Document translation is not supported on this platform.

Limits apply to every newly started document task. The cache stores only successful translated text; failures and suspected untranslated content are not saved.

The app reads the current page text only after you open the built-in reader and explicitly click Extract or Translate. It never reads pages already open in your system browser.

Higher concurrency translates long pages faster but increases momentary load on the provider and may trigger rate limits; use higher values only with a self-hosted or high-quota channel.

When enabled, the reader reads image bytes through the current page session and falls back to a screenshot of the image region when needed. Images that fail recognition, are too small, or exceed the limit are skipped without affecting text translation. Images are processed locally, and OCR results are sent to the selected translation service only while translating the current page.

    Translation Channels

    Channels are tried in the following order by default; you can also choose a preferred API at the bottom of the popup. If the preferred API fails or its circuit breaker opens, the app automatically switches to the next one and keeps your preference for future translations.

    1. Bilibili Index-Translate — enabled by default and free; after AI and before DingTalk
    2. DingTalk — used first when enabled and fully configured
    3. Microsoft Translator — no subscription required; used before free channels such as DeepLX when enabled
    4. Self-hosted DeepLX — configurable and the most stable (recommended)
    5. Public DeepLX — free, may be rate-limited
    6. Google — free; some networks require a proxy
    7. MyMemory — fallback with no registration but a free quota limit

    macOS permissions

    System audio requires Screen Recording permission. Microphone or mixed audio also requires Microphone permission. Live subtitles require macOS 14.2 or later.

    Audio samples are captured locally and sent to the selected speech recognition service; they are not written to disk. Final source text reuses the active multi-channel translator and may incur charges.

    Network Proxy

    Supports HTTP, HTTPS, SOCKS4, and SOCKS5, for example socks5://127.0.0.1:7890.

    Separate multiple rules with semicolons; keep <local> and localhost for a local DeepLX service.

    Service Control

    Stopping the service exits the tray icon and selection translation; restart the app when you need it again.

    Selection Diagnostics

    Records the hit level, duration, and failure reason for each selection. Only the last two days are kept, and no selected text is stored.

    Quick action guide

    Three shortcuts cover different workflows. You can reopen this guide from Settings or the tray menu at any time.

    Alt+T

    Select text to translate

    Select text in any app, then press the translation shortcut to open the translation popup. Best for words, sentences, and paragraphs you can already select.

    Alt+S

    Quick OCR translation

    Press the quick OCR shortcut, drag a box, and release to recognize and translate automatically. Best for images, videos, and text that cannot be selected.

    Alt+O

    Full screenshot editor

    Press the screenshot editor shortcut to open the full screenshot editor. Select an area, then choose Recognize or Translate manually; annotations and image export are also available.
